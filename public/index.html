How does PromptMaster Studio improve a prompt?

It takes three steps and about ten seconds. Paste your prompt, read the lint report, then pick the model you're targeting and get a restructured version back.

  1. 01

    Paste or pick a template

    Drop in any rough prompt, or start from a built-in template. Variables like {{ role }} are detected so you can fill them in live.

    Free
  2. 02

    Lint & score instantly

    The linter flags negative constraints, vague directives, injection risks and broken variables. It scores clarity, security and structure and estimates token cost per model.

    Free, unlimited
  3. 03

    Optimize for your model

    Choose Claude XML, OpenAI chat, Gemini or chain-of-thought and get a structured prompt with clear roles, rules and output format.

    1st full result free · Pro for the rest

    Frequently asked questions

    Short, straight answers. If yours isn't here, ask the support desk or email support@nealfrazier.tech.

    What is PromptMaster Studio?

    A browser-based prompt engineering tool by Neal Frazier Tech. It lints and scores your prompt, estimates token cost, and rewrites it into a structured format for Claude (XML), OpenAI chat, Google Gemini or chain-of-thought reasoning.

    Are my prompts sent to a server?

    No. Linting, scoring and rewriting run in JavaScript inside your browser, and your prompt text is never uploaded. Payments are handled by Stripe or by your own Solana wallet. See the Privacy Policy.

    What can I do for free?

    Almost everything except the full optimizer output. The linter, quality scores, token and cost estimator, prompt diff, version history, templates and curriculum are free and unlimited. Your first optimized prompt is shown in full. After that, each optimized result shows a preview of its first lines.

    What does PromptMaster Studio Pro unlock?

    The complete optimizer. You get the full optimized output for all four targets (Claude XML, OpenAI, Gemini, chain-of-thought), copy and export, and unlimited optimizations in the browser you unlocked.

    How much does Pro cost? Is it a subscription?

    $19, once. It's a single payment for lifetime access to the web studio, with no subscription and no recurring charge.

    What is the refund policy?

    Full refund within 30 days, no questions asked. Email support@nealfrazier.tech with your Stripe receipt email or Solana transaction signature. The full policy is in the Terms of Service, and you can also open a ticket at the support desk.

    Can I pay with crypto?
